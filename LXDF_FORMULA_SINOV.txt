Hisob:

- Ichki eni: (1900 - ((3 + 1) x 16)) / 3 = 612 mm
- Ikki chet: 612 + 22 = 634 mm — 2 ta
- O‘rta: 612 + 14 = 626 mm — 1 ta

Miqdor 6 bo‘lsa:
- 2130 x 634 mm — 12 ta
- 2130 x 626 mm — 6 ta
